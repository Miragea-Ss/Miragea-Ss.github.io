## Objective

- Make the public Infinite Canvas understandable to a first-time visitor without outside documentation.
- Add a fair visual comparison with six referenced open-source projects.
- Explain where Eliora leads for its declared target and where specialist projects remain stronger.

## Deliverables

- A larger, responsive Guide dialog reached from the existing top Help button and `?` shortcut.
- Start Here, How to Use, and Compare tabs in EN and JA.
- Detailed local ComfyUI, workflow JSON, local LLM, BYOK, Agent review, data, export, and troubleshooting instructions.
- A comparison position map, matrix, honest conclusion, and direct source links.

## Constraints

- Preserve the current Eliora/Miragea atmosphere, logo, canvas-first layout, and controls.
- Do not require Chinese real-name verification or any Chinese platform.
- Do not turn the product into a marketing landing page.
- Keep one selected language visible at a time.
- Do not claim universal superiority across products with different purposes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A new visitor can reach the guide from the fixed top navigation and understand the first successful workflow.
2. Every cited comparison project links to its official GitHub repository.
3. The matrix distinguishes production workflow, painting/outpainting, file-format, and rendering-engine strengths.
4. Desktop and 390 px mobile layouts remain usable with no page-level horizontal overflow.
5. Existing contract tests and full site build pass.
